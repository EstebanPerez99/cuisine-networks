People use the word ``authentic'' when discussing online recipes, but ingredient lists and personal judgments may capture different ideas. We measured which ingredient pairs distinguish the cuisine tags on Food.com and gave each recipe a score. Randomizing the recipes showed why this score needs care: much of the separation between cuisines comes from the popularity of individual ingredients, and a pair can distinguish one cuisine without being combined unusually often within it. Comparing pair and single-ingredient scores with review language can show whether these ingredient patterns also match what reviewers call authentic.
