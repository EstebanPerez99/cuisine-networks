\subsection*{Data}
% TODO: one clause on why these nine cuisines.
We used the Food.com Recipes and Interactions dataset, collected by Majumder et al.\ \cite{majumder2019generating} and published on Kaggle in 2019. It is a fixed archive, not a live scrape: 231,637 recipes submitted to Food.com from 1999 to 2018 and 1,132,367 user reviews. We downloaded it in November 2026. We kept recipes tagged with exactly one of nine cuisines: Italian, Mexican, Indian, French, Greek, Middle Eastern, Chinese, Thai, or Japanese. We mapped regional tags to these groups (for example, \emph{szechuan} to Chinese) and removed 431 recipes tagged with two of the nine. We also removed recipes with fewer than two ingredients and exact duplicates with the same name and ingredient set. For the review dataset, we removed 12,981 reviews written for community cooking games, 107 replies from recipe authors, and 18 reviews without text. We treated a rating of zero as missing because it likely meant that no stars were given. The processed dataset has 26,727 recipes and 132,744 reviews.
